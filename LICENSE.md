## 2. What WaveScript Is — and Is Not

WaveScript is a **text formatting and documentation aid**. It is designed to help clinical staff assemble, structure, and dictate free-text EEG reports more quickly, using pre-defined word banks, templates, and optional on-device speech-to-text transcription.

**WaveScript does not:**

- Analyse, interpret, or process EEG signal data in any way.
- Make, suggest, or influence any diagnostic, clinical, or interpretive decision.
- Verify the accuracy, completeness, or clinical validity of any content entered or generated through it.

All clinical content — including any text built from word banks, dictated via speech-to-text, or otherwise entered into the Software — **remains entirely the responsibility of the clinician or author who produces the final report.** The Software is a convenience tool for formatting text; it exercises no clinical judgment and should never be treated as a source of clinical authority. Users must review all generated report content for accuracy before it is finalised, signed, or relied upon clinically.
